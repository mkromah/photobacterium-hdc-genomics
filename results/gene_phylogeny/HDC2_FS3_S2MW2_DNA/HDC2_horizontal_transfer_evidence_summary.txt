1. Species-tree / gene-tree discordance
---------------------------------------
FS3_1 and S2MW2 form a strongly supported HDC2 gene-tree grouping that is
discordant with the UBCG core-genome species tree.

Their smallest common species-tree clade contains 33 of the 36 HDC2-positive
genomes, indicating broad phylogenetic separation in the core-genome tree.

Interpretation
--------------
The combined evidence is strongly consistent with recent mobile-element-
mediated horizontal acquisition of an HDC2-associated genomic region shared
between P. phosphoreum FS3_1 and P. toruni S2MW2.

The evidence includes:
(1) strongly supported HDC2 gene-tree/species-tree discordance,
(2) exceptionally high cross-species HDC2 protein identity,
(3) near-identity across an approximately 16-kb HDC2 locus,
(4) approximately 99.59% identity across 95.78% of the FS3_1 HDC2-bearing
    contig despite only approximately 86.32% whole-genome ANI,
and
(5) an integrase/recombinase-rich pair-specific mobile-element extension.

These observations support horizontal transfer or shared recent acquisition
of the HDC2-associated region, but they do not establish the direction of
transfer or exclude acquisition from an unsampled third donor.
